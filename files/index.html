What this study records

This is a research instrument. Each signal class below can be switched off on its own, and the practice session works exactly the same way with any of them off — measuring that difference is part of the study.

  • Which option you chose and whether it was right. Required: without it there is no adaptive practice to take part in.
  • How long you took to read, to decide and to submit. Used to estimate pace and within-session fatigue.
  • Option changes, idle gaps, and whether the tab was visible and focused. Used to estimate engagement and hesitation.
  • Summary numbers describing the shape of your cursor path — curvature, direction reversals, pauses, hover time per option. The raw path never leaves your browser; only these summaries are stored.
  • Now and then, how sure you were and how hard the item felt. Every one is skippable.
